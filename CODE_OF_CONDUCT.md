# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Sitemap Cohort Auditor to be respectful, useful and safe. Contributors, maintainers and community members are expected to:

- discuss ideas and disagreements without personal attacks;
- give actionable, technically grounded feedback;
- respect different levels of experience, backgrounds and communication styles;
- avoid publishing private information, credentials or third-party data; and
- help keep issue, review and security discussions focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct, doxxing and repeated bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, security reports and other public or private spaces managed for this project. It also applies when someone is representing the project in another community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@edilec.com`. Security vulnerabilities belong in the [private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/edilec/sitemap-cohort-auditor/security/advisories/new), not in a conduct report.

Maintainers will review available context, protect private information where reasonably possible, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include clarification, a warning, content removal, temporary participation limits or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Do not retaliate against someone for raising a good-faith concern.
